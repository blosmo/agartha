# Community code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Give specific, constructive feedback and welcome people with different backgrounds and experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict participation. Enforcement should consider severity, context, and repeated behavior. Maintainers are subject to these expectations too.

Report ordinary moderation concerns to a repository maintainer. For sensitive concerns, ask a maintainer for a private channel without posting personal details. A monitored private conduct contact must be designated before opening community participation. Reports should be handled confidentially to the extent possible; do not promise absolute secrecy where safety requires action.
