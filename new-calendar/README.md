# New Calendar Timepiece

Interactive 3D visualization of Tom Sherman's New Calendar with a Krystal Spiral mechanics overlay.

## What It Shows

- A 365-position New Calendar ring with five 73-day seasons.
- Two 36-day months per season, with a midpoint reflection day.
- Nine-day planetary week rhythm.
- A Krystal Spiral overlay based on the `Spirals of Creation` source model:
  - 45-degree octant steps.
  - `sqrt(2)` expansion per octant.
  - 90-degree quadrant steps.
  - `x2` expansion per quadrant.
  - Mirrored clockwise and counter-clockwise paths.
- A comparison mode that contrasts Krystal `x2` quadrant growth with Golden Mean growth.

## Source Framing

The app treats Tom Sherman's New Calendar as the timekeeping structure and the Krystal Spiral as an interpretive overlay from the supplied Emerald24 / `Spirals of Creation` material. The app labels that layer as source-framed mechanics rather than an empirical astronomy claim.
