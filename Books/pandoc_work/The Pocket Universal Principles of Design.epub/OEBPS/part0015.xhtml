011 Area Alignment
Alignment based on the area of elements versus the edges of elements.
• Asymmetrical elements often do not align properly using conventional edge- or center-based methods.
• In such cases, align elements based on their areas or visual weights, not based on their edges or rectangular centers.
• This technique must be applied using the designer’s eye and judgment, balancing elements on the axis of alignment as if they had mass.
• The principle applies to ragged-edge text blocks as well as graphical elements.
• Align based on area when elements are nonuniform and asymmetrical.
