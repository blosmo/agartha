About the Author

Blue Lily Photography

Austin Kleon is a writer who draws. He is the author of the national bestseller Steal Like an Artist and the redacted poetry collection Newspaper Blackout. He speaks frequently about creativity in the digital age for SXSW, TEDx, The Economist, and more, and his work has been featured in The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, and on NPR and PBS. He lives in Austin, Texas, and online at austinkleon.com.
