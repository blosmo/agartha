028 Design Workshops

Creative work sessions with participants organized around codesign methods

• Gain the creative trust and input of stakeholders and secure buy-in from team members and clients.

• Workshops can consist of projective techniques such as collage, mapping, or diagramming, to gain an understanding of the user’s world.

• There are common in generative research, with participants contributing to ideation through codesign.

• In evaluative sessions, participants collectively review concepts, offer feedback, and contribute insights for design iteration and refinement.

• Workshops may include hands-on training of simple design tools, enabling participants to create mock-ups, sketches, or storyboards, or role-play interactions.

• Plan carefully for a balance of design team facilitators relative to the number of participants, with clearly defined roles.

• These workshops are increasingly used to train interested non-design audiences in the common methods of design research, ideation, thinking, and processes.
