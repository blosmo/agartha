From brief to finished files

01 / Plan

Agree on the scope

Describe the model and intended use. Your submitted budget caps Astra and Blender usage for one managed job. Buy credits separately if needed.

02 / Refine

Build and refine

Start with the main forms, inspect actual previews and fix the most valuable defects. Keep a saved checkpoint. Stop when the brief is met or further edits are unlikely to help.

03 / Deliver

Download and review

Download the model, preview and editable source before closing. Your agent reports what was checked, what remains, the actual cost and why it stopped.

How the costs add up

$0.40 covers the first five running minutes. Each additional begun minute costs $0.05. Prepaid credit options: $5 or $20 USD.

These rates cover the cloud workspace. Managed creation also includes Astra usage within your submitted total cap. Direct Blender uses your own agent provider.

Your agent asks you to approve any prepaid credit purchase separately. Unused credits stay in your wallet and do not count as task usage.

No subscriptions or automatic top-ups. Two CPU cores, 4 GiB RAM, no GPU. Running idle time is billed; automatic shutdown starts after 60 idle seconds. Each resumed session has a new minimum charge.

One budget, several steps

Managed jobs reserve one session of up to 10 minutes, plus Astra usage. Leave room for inspection, fixes and export. Extra time alone does not guarantee a better model.

Keep your model files

Managed jobs include a four-second, 512-pixel, 24 fps 360° video when rendering fits the approved budget. If time or budget runs out, available model files and previews are still delivered with the reason.

Use the exported files in your app, game, website or production pipeline. You can also bring them into Agartha. Managed downloads are retained for seven days after creation; keep your own lasting copies.
