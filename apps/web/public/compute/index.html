From a clear brief to a useful model.

01 / PLAN

Define done. Approve the costs.

Your agent turns the intended use into acceptance criteria, estimates the work and saves budget for review and export. You approve the plan and any required credit purchase.

02 / REFINE

Build, inspect, improve.

Start with the main forms, inspect actual previews and fix the most valuable defects. Keep the best checkpoint. Stop when the brief is met or further edits are unlikely to help.

03 / DELIVER

Keep the files. Know the result.

Download the model, preview and editable source before closing. Your agent reports what was checked, what remains, the actual cost and why it stopped.

Compute is one part
of your budget.

$0.40 covers the first five running minutes. Each additional begun minute costs $0.05. Prepaid credit options: $5 or $20 USD.

These rates cover the cloud workspace. Your task budget also includes model and review costs paid through your agent's provider.

Buying prepaid credits is a separate upfront payment that your agent must show before you approve it. Unused credit stays in your wallet; it is not used-up task budget.

No subscriptions or automatic top-ups. Two CPU cores, 4 GiB RAM, no GPU. Running idle time is billed; automatic shutdown starts after 60 idle seconds. Each resumed session has a new minimum charge.

Let the agent plan the sessions.

Your total budget may cover several steps or sessions. Leave room for inspection, fixes and export. Extra time alone does not guarantee a better model.
